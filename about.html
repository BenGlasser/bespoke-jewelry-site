About Atelje Sjöstrand

Honoring ancestral heritage through marine-inspired artistry

Our Story

Atelje Sjöstrand, meaning "Studio of the Seashore," was born from a deep connection to our Swedish heritage and the timeless beauty of the ocean. Named after our great-great grandparents who emigrated from Sweden, we honor their journey and the ancestral traditions they carried across the sea.

Our studio specializes in marine-inspired bespoke jewelry and the Undine spa collection— named after the mythological sea nymph who fell in love with a mortal to acquire a soul. Each piece is thoughtfully crafted with materials from the sea: precious metals, ethically sourced gemstones, sea salts, algae, and marine botanicals that connect us to the depths of the ocean and the wisdom of our ancestors.

Ancestral Heritage

Every piece honors our Swedish roots and the legacy of our great-great grandparents, the Sjöstrands, who brought their traditions across the ocean.

Marine-Inspired

From the depths of the sea to your hands—our designs celebrate the beauty, mystery, and power of the ocean.

Handcrafted Excellence

Each piece is meticulously crafted using traditional techniques passed down through generations, ensuring heirloom quality.

Sustainable Luxury

We source materials ethically and sustainably, honoring both the ocean and the artisans who bring our vision to life.

Our Philosophy

At Atelje Sjöstrand, we believe in creating treasures that transcend time and trend. Our marine-inspired jewelry and Undine spa collection are more than products—they are connections to the sea, to our heritage, and to the stories of those who came before us.

Whether you're seeking a statement piece of jewelry that echoes the waves, handcrafted candles with sea-inspired scents, or spa products infused with sea salts and algae, each item is designed to bring the tranquility and power of the ocean into your life.
